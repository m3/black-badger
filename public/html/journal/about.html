The Workshop

Gothenburg, Sweden. Where the dust settles and the stories begin.

I started Black Badger with a single goal: to create objects that had no other job than to make people smile. Twenty years later, that goal hasn't changed, but the materials have gotten a lot more interesting.

"I don't think of myself as a jeweler. I'm a guy who likes to see what happens when you push a material to its absolute limit."

Every piece that leaves this bench has been through the forge, the lathe, and my own hands. We don't have a factory. We have a shop. It smells like cutting oil, industrial optimism, and occasionally, burnt fingertips.

The Forty Hours

That's the average time it takes to build a piece from scratch. It's not efficient. It's not scalable. But it's the only way I know how to make something that feels real.

Honest Materials

Whether it's Fordite from old Detroit auto plants or Damascus steel forged by Chris Ploof, we only work with materials that have a history. We don't hide the grain. We show the work.
