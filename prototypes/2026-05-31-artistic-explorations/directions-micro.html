The 20th Anniversary Ring

James Thompson on the materials, the mistakes, and the moment he knew the 20th Anniversary Ring was done.

The ring started as a sketch on a napkin in a Gothenburg bar in 2006. Just a circle with two words: "last forever." Twenty years later, that napkin became the 20th Anniversary Ring — tungsten core, lume inlay, serial numbered.

"I didn't set out to make a commemorative piece. I set out to make the ring I'd want to wear every single day. The anniversary part came later."

What makes this ring different from the previous 19 iterations isn't the materials — it's the patience. Thompson spent three months on the lume channeling alone, layering the compound by hand until it glowed the exact shade of the midnight sea outside his workshop.
