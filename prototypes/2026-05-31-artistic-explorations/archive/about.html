Engineering Mind

A deep dive into the technical methodology of James Thompson and the foundation of the Black Badger workshop.

Technical Archeology

James Thompson doesn't just design jewelry; he excavates industrial history. By working with materials like Fordite—layers of compressed auto paint from Detroit—he treats every piece as a core sample of time.

The Archive track focuses on this analytical approach. It is where the forge meets the drafting table, and where metallurgical limits are pushed to their breaking point.

"If Black Badger has a 'thing,' it's that there's always something in there that tells a story."

Material Logic

Whether it's Zirconium reacting to fire or Damascus steel revealing its folded patterns through acid etching, the process is governed by physical laws. Our workshop in Gothenburg is a laboratory where these laws are tested daily.

We don't polish away the evidence of the making. We celebrate the marks of the tool and the grain of the alloy. This is honest design.
