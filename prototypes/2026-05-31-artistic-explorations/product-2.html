The Black Badger
20th Anniversary Ring

Zirconium · Hand-forged · Gothenburg, SE

It's been 20 years since James started Black Badger. From fun little objects to a highly innovative contributor to the Swiss watch industry. This ring is the physical artifact of that journey — a piece of the Black Badger universe you can wear on your finger.

Zirconium behaves differently under a torch — it's like painting with fire. The surface captures light in ways that feel almost alive. Each one is unique because each one was made by hand, by James, in his Gothenburg workshop.

Zirconium — The Element That Paints With Fire

Zirconium is a beast. Under a torch, it doesn't just heat — it blooms. The surface oxidizes in layers of gold, blue, amber, and deep violet. You can't fully control it. You guide it, and it decides.

James has been working with zirconium since the early days of Black Badger. It's the material that taught him that the best pieces aren't the ones you plan perfectly — they're the ones where you let the material have a say.

Raw zirconium billet, before torch treatment. The colors come from the heat — no dyes, no coatings. Just fire and metal.
