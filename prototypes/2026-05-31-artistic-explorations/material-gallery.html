Chapter 04 — The Material Gallery

Twenty Years of Finding
the Extraordinary

Compressed auto paint. Meteorite. Damascus steel forged by hand. Zirconium that catches fire. Every ring tells the story of where it came from.

↓ Scroll to explore

It started with a conversation about materials that shouldn't exist. Fordite is what happens when you spray 80 years of car paint on the same wall in the same factory. Damascus is what happens when you fold steel 300 times by hand. Zirconium is what happens when you put a torch to a metal that doesn't want to sit still.

I don't design rings. I find materials that have something to say, and then I get out of the way.

— James Thompson, Black Badger
